Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we handle your data.

Effective Date: July 15, 2026
1

Introduction

Welcome to Smart Notes AI ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Smart Notes AI (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

2

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Email address — used for account creation and authentication
  • Display name — used to personalize your experience
  • Profile picture — obtained through Google Sign-In (if applicable)

User-Generated Content

  • Notes and text content — created and stored within the App
  • Categories and tags — used to organize your notes
  • Audio recordings — when using voice-to-text features

Usage Data

  • AI feature usage — to manage your quota and improve our services
  • App preferences — such as theme settings
3

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your user account
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
  • To enable AI-powered features such as text summarization, translation, and voice-to-text conversion
  • To sync your notes and data across sessions
  • To manage subscription tiers and usage quotas
  • To send important service-related communications (e.g., password reset emails)
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues
4

Google Sign-In

Our App offers Google Sign-In as an authentication option. When you sign in with Google, we receive access to your basic profile information (name, email, and profile picture) as authorized by your Google account settings.

We only request the minimum scopes necessary: email and profile. We do not access your Google contacts, calendar, drive, or any other Google services.

5

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers powered by PocketBase. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • All data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Access to user data is restricted to authenticated users only
  • We perform regular security assessments of our infrastructure
6

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 — for authentication purposes
  • AI/LLM Services — for text summarization, translation, and voice-to-text processing. Text content sent to AI services is processed in real-time and is not permanently stored by these providers.
  • SMTP Email Service — for sending OTP codes and password reset emails

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

7

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users and the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with prior notice)
8

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — You can view your personal information within the App
  • Correction — You can update your profile information at any time
  • Deletion — You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Export — You can request a copy of your data
9

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10

Changes to This Policy
